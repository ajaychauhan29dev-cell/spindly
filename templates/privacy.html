Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to authenticate you, to store and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you request. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and kept for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. We do not otherwise share your data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please contact the Spendly team through the contact details provided on our website.
